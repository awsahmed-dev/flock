Beyond hotels: what else the Demand API offers

  • Attractions: tickets and tours for Discover's "things to do". This would replace the GetYourGuide idea with one partner.
  • Car rentals: a "Getting around" card on days between cities. This fits Jeddah → Riyadh.
  • Flights: search only. Sawia already reads tickets you upload, so this is the least urgent.
  • Reviews: score breakdowns and review text in the hotel sheet.

What it takes to get here

  • CJ approval (pending) turns on tier 1 links. That's the switch that's built and off today.
  • Managed Affiliate Partner: apply to Booking.com directly. An account manager enables API keys. There's no self-serve signup, and it usually takes real traffic first.
  • Search, Look & Book: a second approval with a business case, for tier 2.
  • AI sign-off: Booking.com's partner terms need written approval before an AI system uses their data. The AI planner couldn't pick hotels from these results until that's granted. Ranking by distance to your stops is plain maths, not AI.

What makes it Sawia, not a Booking.com clone

Ranking by your plan. Booking.com knows the city. Sawia knows the six places you're going, so "near 4 of your stops" beats any star filter.

Priced for the crew. Rooms come from the crew size, and every price shows per person. A group compares the per-person number, not the total.

Decided together. Crew hearts show on each hotel, and a vote goes through the existing votes feature. The card says "Crew voting" so nobody double-books.

Honesty rules

Prices change, so the card says whose prices they are and for which dates. The price is re-checked when a hotel is opened. We never show a price as booked until a confirmation says so.

Sort order is ours, and commission never changes it. "Sawia may earn a commission" sits under every list that links out, same as today.
