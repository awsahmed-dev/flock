How a Stays booking works

From “nobody's booked Jeddah” to a commission in your CJ account: every hop, what each party learns, and the two values CJ will send you. Your real trip is used throughout — Jeddah, 6–11 October, one of you.

3
Sawia · the moment you tap “Find stays on Booking.com”

Writes down the tap, tells the crew, and hands you on

The button doesn't point at Booking. It points at Sawia's own server route, which does three things in a few milliseconds:

① logs the click — which trip, which city, which button, when — so it can later be matched against CJ's report;
② marks the stay “looking”, so anyone else in the trip sees “Aws is checking stays” instead of booking a second hotel;
③ redirects you to CJ.

CJ Affiliate · invisible, under a second

Records the click and sets its cookie

You never see this page. CJ notes the click under Sawia's reference code — sawia-stays-bc506179 — sets a cookie so the booking can be credited to Sawia, and redirects you on.

Booking.com

A search that's already filled in

Jeddah, 6–11 October, 1 adult, 1 room, prices in SAR, in Arabic. You browse, compare and book exactly as you normally would — your name, card and email go to Booking, never through Sawia.

7
CJ Affiliate · days to weeks later

The commission shows up

Booking.com reports the booking to CJ. It appears in your CJ dashboard as 4% of the booking, in USD, under the reference sawia-stays-bc506179 — which is how you know it came from the Stays card on that trip. Booking pays on completed stays, so a cancelled booking earns nothing. CJ then pays out on its own schedule.

The link, hop by hop

Values in orange are the ones CJ gives you on approval. Everything else Sawia fills in per tap.

①What the button points at — Sawia's own route
GET /api/affiliate/stays?trip=bc506179-…&base=jeddah

Nothing about CJ is in the page, so the IDs can't be copied out of it — and every tap is logged before it leaves.

②Where the server sends you — CJ's tracking link
https://www.{cj-domain}/click-{PID}-{AD_ID}?sid=sawia-stays-bc506179&url={③, encoded}

The shape of a CJ deep link. The exact domain and IDs arrive with approval — this is the BOOKING_LINK_TEMPLATE setting, so switching it on is a config change, not a rewrite. sid is Sawia's reference code: the only thread tying a CJ report back to a trip, so it carries no name or email — just which button and the first 8 characters of the trip's ID.

③Where CJ sends you — the Booking.com search
https://www.booking.com/searchresults.html?ss=Jeddah&checkin=2026-10-06&checkout=2026-10-11&group_adults=1&no_rooms=1&selected_currency=SAR&lang=ar

One change from the original plan, which assumed a direct Booking.com account: through CJ, attribution is CJ's job, so Sawia doesn't set Booking's own aid or label. The reference moves to CJ's sid in ②.

One switch, three positions

off today

Stays cards show as plain reminders — “you need a bed in Jeddah, nobody's claimed it” — with no button. Still useful; nothing depends on approval.

preview

The button works and goes straight to the filled-in Booking.com search, skipping CJ. No tracking, no commission. For testing the whole flow before approval.

live

Through CJ, earning. Needs the two values CJ sends on approval. Flipping it is a settings change, not a new release.
