01 · Brand

Story

Paxawa exists because planning a group trip should feel like excitement, not a second job. We are the quiet infrastructure behind your loudest adventures.

Paxawa exists because planning a group trip should feel like excitement, not a second job. We believe the moment you decide to travel with people you love should be the best part — not the weeks of back-and-forth messages, forgotten spreadsheets, and unresolved debates that follow.

The name Paxawa is a coined word — short, memorable, and globally pronounceable. It carries no baggage. It's new, like every trip you'll plan with it.

Our brand reflects the product: calm confidence, clarity at every step, and a warmth that makes complex things feel effortless.

Calm confidence
We handle complexity so you don't feel it. Confident and grounded, never overwhelming.
Collective joy
Travel is better together. Our design celebrates the group, not just the individual.
Radical clarity
Every screen answers one question clearly. We never bury the next step.
Genuine warmth
We're not sterile software. We're the friend who organises the trip — and enjoys it too.
02 · Brand

Logo system

A compass-pin in a dome — paths converging on a destination. Use the lockup at the size required, and never modify the proportions.

Anatomy
Symbol mark
A stylised compass / navigation pin inside a dome. The X-shape is intersecting paths — people converging from different directions. The dome evokes a destination, a shelter, a meeting point.
Wordmark
Set in Sora ExtraBold. Tight tracking and heavy weight communicate confidence; lowercase setting keeps it approachable.
Clear space
Maintain minimum clear space equal to the cap-height of the letter "P" on all four sides of the lockup.

06 · Expression

Voice & tone

We write like a friend who's organised this trip before. Helpful without being chatty, confident without being cold, specific without being precious.

Brand attributes
Calm Clear Warm Clever (not cute) Direct Human Confident
Yes / no
✓ Paxawa sounds like this
"Plan the trip. Not the group chat."
"Maya paid $480. You owe $120."
"Beachfront wins. Added to Day 2."
"One link. Your whole crew."
"Trip exported. Safe travels ✈"
✗ Paxawa never sounds like this
"We've leveraged AI to optimise your collaborative workflow."
"Congratulations! You successfully logged an expense!"
"Uh oh! Something went wrong. Please try again later."
"Are you absolutely sure you want to do this?"
Writing rules
✓ Do
Use sentence case everywhere — only proper nouns get capitals.
"Start your trip" not "Start Your Trip"
✗ Don't
Never use corporate jargon: leverage, synergy, optimise workflow, seamless.
✓ Do
Lead with the outcome. State what happened, then what it means.
"Paid · $120 split 4 ways" not "Expense logging complete"
✗ Don't
Don't write error messages that blame. Explain what happened and offer a next step.
✓ Do
Be specific with numbers. Use real values where data is available.
"You owe $84" not "You owe some money"
✗ Don't
Don't over-celebrate with exclamation marks or emoji clusters.
Avoid: "Amazing! You did it! 🎉🎉🎉"
07 · Expression

Motion

Motion is purposeful, not decorative. Every animation communicates state or guides attention. Hover a row to play the easing curve.

Instant
0ms
Checkbox toggles, radio buttons — immediate feedback required.
Micro
100ms · ease-out
Button press, icon swap, focus ring appearance.
Fast
150ms · ease-out
Hover states, badge updates, inline validation feedback.
Default
200ms · ease-in-out
Most UI: dropdowns, tab switches, card reveals.
Deliberate
300ms · cubic-bezier
Sheets, modals, page transitions, vote bar fills.
Expressive
500ms · spring
Vote close reveal, expense logged celebration — rare and intentional.
Motion principles
Animate GPU-friendly properties only: opacity and transform (translate, scale, rotate). Avoid animating width, height, padding, or margin.
Reduced motion
Always respect prefers-reduced-motion. Use opacity-only fade as a universal fallback for all positional animations.
